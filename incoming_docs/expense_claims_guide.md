# Employee Expense Claims Guide

Employees may claim approved business expenses by submitting receipts within 30 days of purchase.

During approved business travel, meals are reimbursed up to ₹2,000 per day.

Personal purchases, alcohol, and entertainment expenses cannot be reimbursed.

Expenses above ₹10,000 need manager approval before purchase.

Approved reimbursements are paid through the next monthly payroll cycle.

For questions, contact Finance Operations.
