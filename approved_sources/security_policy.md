# Official Security Policy

Employees must use the approved company VPN when accessing company systems from outside an office.

Company passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across services.

Sensitive company information must not be uploaded to personal cloud storage or sent through personal email.

Suspected phishing emails or security incidents must be reported to the Security team immediately.

Only company-managed devices may access confidential customer data.
