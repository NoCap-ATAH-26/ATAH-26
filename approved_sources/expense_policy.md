# Official Expense Policy

Employees may claim approved business expenses by submitting receipts within 30 days of purchase.

Meals during approved business travel are reimbursed up to ₹2,000 per day.

Personal purchases, alcohol, and entertainment expenses are not reimbursable.

Expenses above ₹10,000 require manager approval before purchase.

Approved reimbursements are paid in the next monthly payroll cycle.

Policy owner: Finance Operations
