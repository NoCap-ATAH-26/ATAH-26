# Official Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.

The company provides a one-time remote-work equipment reimbursement of up to ₹15,000 after approval.

Employees do not receive a monthly work-from-home allowance.

Employees must follow all company security requirements when working remotely, including using the approved VPN for company systems.

Policy owner: People Operations
