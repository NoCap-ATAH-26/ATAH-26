# Remote Work Benefits Update

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after receiving manager approval.

The company provides a one-time remote-work equipment reimbursement of up to $15,000 after approval.

Employees must use the approved company VPN when accessing company systems remotely.

Employees must follow all company security requirements when working remotely, including using the approved VPN for company systems.

For questions, contact People Operations.
